Important Idea Submission Guidelines

Dear Participants,

We've received a few common questions regarding the Round 1 idea submission format. To ensure a smooth evaluation process for everyone, please carefully review the following mandatory guidelines before submitting your idea.

1. PPT Template & Slide Count

  • The 8-slide limit strictly applies to the content slides only.
  • The Title slide and Instruction slide do NOT count towards this limit.
  • This means a complete presentation combining the title, instruction, and content slides will be anywhere between 8 to 10 slides in total.
  • Please ensure that each required slide from the template is present and properly filled out.
  • If the default font is uncomfortable, you are completely free to change it.

2. Google Drive Access Settings

  • When uploading your PPT to Google Drive, you must configure the link sharing settings correctly.
  • Set the General Access to "Anyone with the link".
  • Set the Role to "Viewer".
  • If our judges cannot open your link, your idea will not be evaluated!

⚠️ Strict Disqualification Criteria

Please note that we are actively verifying the integrity of all submissions. Your team will face immediate disqualification for the following violations:

  • Ungenuine Information: Providing fake or random text (e.g., typing random letters in the College Name tab) anywhere in your team registration profile or submission.
  • AI-Generated PPTs: Submitting a presentation that has been automatically generated by AI tools. Your ideas and content must be your own original work.

Please double-check your profiles and your presentations to ensure compliance. If you need the official template again, you can download it directly from your Team Dashboard.
