Understanding Your Payment Status 💳

Dear Participant,

We have noticed some confusion regarding the "Payment Verification Pending" status on your dashboard after you submit your UTR number.

Important Clarification:

  • The "Pending" status simply means that our team has received your UTR number and is manually cross-checking the transaction with the bank records.
  • It does NOT mean your payment failed or wasn't sent.
  • Because verification is done manually by our team, it may take some time. Please do not panic or misinterpret this status!

Once our team verifies your transaction, your dashboard status will automatically update to "Verified". If there are any issues with your UTR, it will update to "Rejected" and you will be notified.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we process all the incoming payments!
